About

The studio

A one-person studio focused on short, polished mobile games.

Who makes it

Lord Lima is Gabriel's game development studio. Each project is designed, programmed and published by one person, from the first sprite to the store release.

How it works

  • Unity 6, Android first
  • Pixel art and readable UI
  • Games in Portuguese, English and Spanish
  • A clear privacy policy for every app

Tools

Games are made in Unity 6 for Android, with pixel art created in Aseprite. Ads use Unity LevelPlay and online progress uses Firebase and Google Play Games.

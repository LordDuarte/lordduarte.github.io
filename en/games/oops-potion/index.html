Oops Potion

Mix, nail it, discover!

PRIVACY POLICY

How to play

Pick up to 4 different ingredients, pour the liquids and throw the solids into the cauldron, then stir for 5 seconds. If the combination is right the potion is discovered, and its quality, from 0 to 100%, depends on how close you got to the doses. If it is wrong, the game gives a hint about what is missing. Watch out for explosive ingredients: stir too long and the cauldron blows up.

What is in the game

  • 40 recipes, from Common to Epic, to record in your book
  • 17 effects you only find out by drinking: horns, wings, a burning hat, rainbow hair
  • Recipes where ingredient order matters
  • Quality in four tiers, from Weak Potion to Perfect Potion
  • Coins for potion quality and an ingredient shop

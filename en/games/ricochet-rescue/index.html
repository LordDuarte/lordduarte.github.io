Ricochet Rescue

Save the hostages in the Wild West.

GET ITPRIVACY POLICY

How to play

Hold, drag and release to aim. The bullet passes through ropes, friends and enemies and is only stopped by surfaces, so use the walls and bounce your shots. Free every friend and take down every enemy to clear a level. If the cowboy or a friend dies, or time or bullets run out, the level is lost.

What is in the game

  • 66 levels in two worlds: Wild West I and Wild West II (Railroad, Swamp and Ice)
  • Three stars per level, based on bullets used
  • Special ammo, chests and a shop
  • Bonus levels with bottle shooting and duck hunting
  • Shooting enemies, moving platforms, powder barrels, shields and bosses
  • Text in Portuguese, English and Spanish

Game facts

  • 66levels
  • ★★★per level
  • 2worlds

Platform: Android
Engine: Unity 6
Price: Free, with ads and an ad-free option
